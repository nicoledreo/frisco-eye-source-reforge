Frisco, TX Optometrist

Carey Brooks, OD

Dr. Carey Brooks is a graduate of the Texas A&M University Class of 2003. While at Texas A&M University, Dr. Brooks majored in Biology and minored in Business. She then went on to further her education at the University of Houston, College of Optometry where she graduated with a Doctor of Optometry degree in May of 2008. She has been practicing Optometry in the North Texas area since that time.

.

Book an Eye Exam Call 214-872-2400

Dr. Brooks has extensive training in all fields of Optometry and had the incredible opportunity to learn and train with some of the most influential Optometric Physicians in the nation. She specializes in refractive techniques and ocular pathology. Dr. Brooks is a Therapeutic Optometrist and Glaucoma Specialist. She diagnoses, treats and manages many different ocular conditions including: Refractive conditions, dry eye syndrome, macular degeneration, glaucoma, cataracts, eye infections, diabetic retinopathy, hypertensive retinopathy, corneal dystrophies and many other anterior and posterior ocular pathologies. She also has an interest in specialty contact lens fitting including: Orthokeratology, scleral contact lenses and multifocal contact lenses.

Dr. Brooks has the distinct privilege of co-managing LASIK and cataract surgery with some of the most renowned surgeons in North Texas. Her relationship with these surgeons helps to ensure that her patients receive the best care possible when pursing all types of ocular surgery.

Dr. Brooks has the most wonderful husband, Craig, and three amazing children, Ella, Garrett and Caleb. She loves spending time with her family and friends and attending her children’s functions. She cannot wait to meet with you and provide you with the best optometric care possible.
