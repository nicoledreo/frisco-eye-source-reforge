Eye Care Services

Our clinic offers a full range of eye health services to clients of all ages, from children to older adults: routine eye exams and vision tests, eye disease diagnosis and management, immediate attention for eye emergencies, and complete eye health management. Our optometry practice in Frisco, TX provides eyewear prescriptions and offers corrective laser eye surgery co-management as well. You can get in touch with our office to set up an appointment at your convenience and learn more about some of our key services below.

Comprehensive Eye Exams in Frisco, TX 75034 & So Much More!

Our Eye Care Services:

  • Emergency Eye Care Services Our eye doctors have the latest technology and lots of experience with eye infections, scratched eye, something stuck in your eye, painful or stinging eyes and many other types of eye infections.
  • Comprehensive Eye Exams Complete eye exams are not just for updating your prescription. Learn more about what your eye doctor will screen for, what to expect at your appointment, some of the advanced technology we use, and special considerations for pediatric and infant eye care.
  • OptiLight and OptiPlus OptiLight is a revolutionary light-based treatment designed to address various ocular conditions, including dry eye disease. Using innovative and precise light therapy, OptiLight improves tear production and reduces inflammation, providing long-lasting relief for patients.
  • Contact Lens Exams A routine eye examination will not provide some of the measurements and testing that are required to determine if your eyes are suitable for contact lens wear. Therefore, our contact lens examinations provide the in depth measurements needed to ensure the best fit of your contact lenses. We pride ourselves on being able to provide even "hard-to-fit" eyes with successful contact lens fits and experiences.
  • Eye Condition Treatment Eye problems can range from mild to severe; some are chronic, while others may resolve on their own. Here's a short list of common eye conditions we treat, such as astigmatism, dry eye syndrome and presbyopia.
  • Eye Disease Management We diagnose and manage ocular diseases such as Glaucoma, Macular Degeneration, Diabetic Retinopathy and Cataracts.
  • Eye Emergencies (Pink/Red Eyes) Did you know your optometrist can help you with red eyes, pink eye, sore eyes, foreign body removal and eye emergencies?
  • LASIK & Refractive Surgery Co-Management If you're ready for an alternative to glasses and/or contacts look to us for co-management of LASIK, cataract, and other ocular surgery.
  • Latisse Try Latisse™ for longer, thicker lashes.
  • Your Eye Health We invite you to browse through these articles to find information about vision and eye health for you and your family.
